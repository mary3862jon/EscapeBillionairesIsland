# PERKS.md — Hidden Spoon Powers

> Boba's brief: hidden perks scattered around the island, hard to find. Each one unlocks an ability that turns Sir Spoonacci from "ornamental cutlery" into "ornamental cutlery that can ruin your week." Every offensive perk leaves a **Spoon Mark** on the victim — a curved silhouette burn/bruise on their forehead, persistent and humiliating.

---

## Perk Slots

A spoon has **4 perk slots** (one per direction: head/handle/bowl/edge). Each found perk fills one slot. Slots can be re-assigned at **Salon Cucchiaio** (the salon doubles as the loadout screen — themed as "spa treatment menu").

## The 4 Launch Perks

| Perk | Verb | Best Against | Spoon-Mark Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🪶 **Featherspoon** | Fly (glide + boost) | Traversal, escape | n/a (utility) |
| 🦵 **Iron Toe-Spoon** | Kick | Crates, doors, shins | grey-blue bruise |
| 🪞 **Bonkmaster** | Head-bonk | Drunk guards, celebrities | red goose-egg + spoon outline |
| 🍑 **Cheek Tenderizer** | Spank | Yacht staff, pranksters | crimson handprint-shaped spoon |

## Perk 1 — Featherspoon (FLIGHT)

**Hidden:** Inside **The Temple** (Act 4 unlock area). On the altar, behind the "Ceremonial Champagne Bowl." To reach it: solve the **Pillar Riddle** — 4 pillars that play notes when you hit them with the spoon; correct sequence (low-low-high-low, hinted by a stained-glass window depicting an Italian opera) lowers the altar.

**Ability:**
- Hold **Space** in mid-air → spoon angles like a glider, slow descent
- Tap **Space + W** while gliding → small forward boost (charges from collecting champagne bubbles in the open island)
- 3-second cooldown between boosts

**Visual:** spoon trails a tiny napkin-cape that becomes parachute-shaped mid-glide.

**Unlock dialogue:** *"SIR SPOONACCI: They threw me away from the heavens. Now I borrow them back."*

---

## Perk 2 — Iron Toe-Spoon (KICK)

**Hidden:** Buried beneath the **Helipad**, marked by a suspicious patch of darker sand. Three things must happen at once:
1. Spank the lazy gardener (perk-spank required → chicken-and-egg, *or* press E near him 5× to enrage)
2. He stomps off, dropping his **rake**
3. Use the rake (interact) to dig the helipad sand

**Ability:**
- Press **F** to perform a roundhouse kick. Spoon spins on its handle, bowl-end strikes.
- Knocks back doors, breakable crates, drunk guards, and small dogs (which then bark hilariously and run away unharmed — Steam-safe).
- Cooldown 1.2s. Combo-chains into Bonk for +damage.

**Spoon Mark:** grey-blue boot-shaped bruise on the shin of whoever you kick. Stays for the rest of the play session.

## Perk 3 — Bonkmaster (HEAD BONK)

**Hidden:** Inside **The Kitchen** (irony intentional — the place that used to abuse spoons). Find the **Walk-In Pantry**, jump on the top shelf (requires Featherspoon glide OR a stacked crate puzzle), discover the **Sacred Mortar & Pestle** shrine. Bonk the pestle 3 times to summon the perk.

**Ability:**
- Press **Q** to swing the spoon overhead like a hammer. Bowl-down impact.
- Stuns humans for 4 seconds, leaves Spoon Mark.
- Against billionaires: triggers a comedic "I'm gonna sue you for everything you OOOH MY ANCESTORS" reaction.

**Spoon Mark:** vivid red goose-egg with a perfect spoon-bowl outline burned into the forehead. Persistent. Visible from across the map.

**Achievement tie-in:** "Bonk Voyage" (bonk every billionaire at least once).

---

## Perk 4 — Cheek Tenderizer (SPANK)

**Hidden:** Inside the **Spa & Massage Dungeon** (subterranean wing the billionaires don't talk about — themed dark but Steam-safe: no human victims visible, just empty massage tables and creepy candlesticks). Survive the **Towel Maze** (rotating towel-rack puzzle, ~3 min). At the center: a single golden spanking paddle on a velvet cushion. Bow to it (hold Tab), it dissolves into the spoon.

**Ability:**
- Press **R** near an NPC's back side → satisfying *thwack*. Spoon arcs in a wide spank motion.
- NPC yelps, hops in pain, drops whatever they were carrying (great for stealing keys / champagne / contraband).
- Long cooldown (3s) but stacks with Bonk for combo "**The Sundae Special**."

**Spoon Mark:** crimson handprint-shape, but spoon-bowl-shaped, on the rear of the victim. Lingering crimson glow.

**Trolling marketing hook:** screenshot mode highlights the marks — players post compilation clips.

## Stacking & Combos

| Combo | Inputs | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **The Sundae Special** | Bonk → Spank within 1.5s | Bonk-stuns, then spanks while stunned. Spoon Marks on both forehead AND rear. |
| **Helipad Hellraiser** | Glide + Kick | Glide downward, kick midair, knocks NPC into water |
| **Tea-Party Terror** | Stack 4 NPCs near a table, Bonk one | Chain-reaction wobble, all spill champagne onto themselves |

## Unlock Order (Designer's Recommended)

1. **Bonk** first — easiest puzzle, gives players a power fantasy fast
2. **Spank** second — combo opens up
3. **Kick** third — unlocks helipad escape route to other zones
4. **Featherspoon** last — endgame mobility, late reward

## Notes

- All Spoon Marks are **purely visual humiliation** — no blood, no gore. Slapstick only. Crucial for Steam approval and "T for Teen" target rating.
- NPCs with Spoon Marks have unique voice barks: *"I shall have my lawyers tenderize you!"*, *"The bouncer didn't see this coming!"*, *"Mum, the cutlery is uprising!"*
- Spoon Marks fade after exiting the island and re-entering (otherwise screenshots would get cluttered after 5 hours).
