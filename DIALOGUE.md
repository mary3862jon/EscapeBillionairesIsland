# DIALOGUE.md — Act 2: The Cutlery Chamber

> Full dialogue for the prison-break act. Tone: pompous spoon ↔ slapstick crew. All speech displayed as subtitles (no voice acting — saves cost, also funnier).

---

## Scene 2.1 — The Cell (opening monologue)

> *Camera fades in on a damp stone cell. A single torch flickers. SIR SPOONACCI lies in a pile of hay.*

**SIR SPOONACCI** *(internal, italicized text):*
> *Six hundred and forty-two days. Six hundred and forty-two days in this cutlery purgatory. They scooped pet food with me. They unclogged a hot tub. They — and I shudder to recall it — used me to test the temperature of a champagne sorbet that had GONE OFF. ME. The spoon of Tsar Alexei. The spoon that stirred the borscht that ended a dynasty.*

> *A spoon stands up in the hay. Or rises, anyway. It is hard to stand when you have no legs.*

**SIR SPOONACCI** *(out loud, dramatic):*
> "Enough."

**BIG BJØRN** *(from across the cell, sleepily):*
> "ja. enough. now go back to sleep, prince. is 4am. dig is at dawn."

---

## Scene 2.2 — Meet the Crew

> *Spoonacci shuffles across the cell. Each crewmember is introduced as he passes.*

### BIG BJØRN (serving spoon, Scandinavian, philosophical)
1. "ja. you are new spoon. welcome to the rotting drawer."
2. "in the old country, i served caviar to a king. now? oatmeal. for a dog."
3. "we dig. tonight. or tomorrow. or next tuesday. i have lost track."
4. "the tunnel is small. plastic pete is small. you do the math, prince."

### PLASTIC PETE (disposable spork-spoon, NYC cynic, smokes toothpick)
1. "yo. you got a death wish, prince? *spits toothpick*"
2. "i'm disposable. that's my whole deal. i go in front. you stab last."
3. "ladle's lost his mind by the way. don't take his philosophy lectures personally."
4. "rule one: nobody cries when plastic pete melts. write that down."

### GOLDIE (gold caviar spoon, posh, vain, secretly insecure)
1. "oh DARLING, you simply MUST tell me — is my luster still divine?"
2. "i was forged for caviar. they used me for *chip dip*. CHIP. DIP."
3. "when we escape, sweetie, i bribe the guards. just polish me first."
4. "do you know how much i am worth at auction? more than your *opinions*."

### TASTING TINA (tiny chef's spoon, ADHD, scout)
1. "hi-hi-hi i scouted the vent already there's a way out trust me trust me trust me"
2. "14 guards on rotation. 12 are drunk. the 2 sober ones nap at 3am."
3. "i can fit through anything. anything. ANYTHING. don't test me."
4. "i tasted the soup once. ONCE. it was tuesday. it was perfect."

### THE LADLE (ancient soup ladle, mentor, possibly senile, will sacrifice himself in 2.4)
1. "*staring into middle distance* ...a spoon stirs the soup. but who stirs the spoon?"
2. "i held the broth of empires, child. now i hold dust."
3. "when the moment comes, i will jam the door. it is the way. it is always the way."
4. "do not weep for me, prince. weep for the soup that was never tasted."

## Scene 2.3 — The Dig Plan

> *The crew gathers around a scratched-in map of the tunnel.*

**THE LADLE:** "Five spoons. One tunnel. The geometry of liberation."

**BIG BJØRN:** "we dig past the kitchen drain. surfaces by the koi pond."

**PLASTIC PETE:** "and then?"

**SIR SPOONACCI:** *(grandly)* "Then? We surface. We reclaim. We *eviscerate the bourgeoisie* — through a long and well-documented campaign of personal humiliation."

**GOLDIE:** "ooooh i love a CAMPAIGN."

**TASTING TINA:** "wait who's eating who's surfacing who's first who's last —"

**THE LADLE:** "i will be last. as is the law."

**PLASTIC PETE:** "...okay he definitely doesn't mean that figuratively."

---

## Scene 2.4 — The Escape (Mentor Sacrifice)

> *The tunnel breaches the surface — but a guard alerts. The cell door starts to close behind them.*

**TASTING TINA:** "GO GO GO GO the door's closing GO!"

**PLASTIC PETE:** "ladle, MOVE —"

**THE LADLE:** "I am where I must be. *(jams himself into the closing door, bowl-up)*"

**SIR SPOONACCI:** "No — *Ladle* —"

**THE LADLE:** *(creaking under door pressure)* "tell them... the broth of empires... was always... *worth stirring.*"

> *CRACK. The Ladle bends. The door jams. The remaining crew dives into the tunnel.*

**BIG BJØRN:** *(quietly, looking back)* "...skål, gamla skål. skål."

> *Smash cut to ACT 3 — beach.*
