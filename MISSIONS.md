# MISSIONS.md — Act 4 Sandbox Quest List

> The "Humiliation Quests" — one per major villain + side jobs. Sandbox = pick any order. Each gives a story unlock + cosmetic + (sometimes) a perk slot.

---

## Main Quests — One Per Billionaire

### M01 — *Tusk, Tusk* (Magnus Tusk)
**Premise:** Magnus is preparing to livestream a rocket launch from his beachside pad. His prized falcon sits on a perch.

**Objective:** Replace the rocket's payload (a vanity NFT) with a single Plastic Pete. Magnus launches "the future of cutlery into low Earth orbit." Stream chat goes viral.

**Reward:** Skin — *Rocket Booster Bowl*. Achievement: *"To the Moon (literally)."*

---

### M02 — *Yacht to Be Kidding* (Beff Jezos)
**Premise:** Beff's mile-long yacht hosts a champagne tower party.

**Objective:** Sneak into the kitchen, swap all champagne with seawater, and Bonk Beff into the tower as the toast begins. Glass cascade. Pearl earrings ruined.

**Reward:** Spank perk slot unlocked (if not already). Achievement: *"Burst Bubbles."*

---

### M03 — *Zucker Punch* (Mark Zuckersnort)
**Premise:** Mark is conducting "human research" — measuring guests' reactions to AI-generated jokes.

**Objective:** Hijack his research tablet (kick it out of his hands), replace all AI jokes with **The Ladle's late-stage philosophy quotes**. Mark gives a 4-minute keynote that breaks his audience emotionally.

**Reward:** Skin — *Hooded Hoodie Spoon*. Achievement: *"Pivot to Cutlery."*

---

### M04 — *Bronson's Babes* (Sir Ricky Bronson)
**Premise:** Sir Ricky is hosting an "experience" — a hot-air balloon ride for influencers.

**Objective:** Steal the balloon's anchor with Iron Toe-Spoon. Balloon drifts gently into a flock of seagulls. Influencers livestream the chaos.

**Reward:** Skin — *Aviator Edition* (with tiny goggles on the bowl). Achievement: *"Flight Risk."*

---

### M05 — *Chad's Greatest Loss* (Crypto Chad)
**Premise:** Chad maintains an "elite NFT vault" — physical USB sticks in a glass case.

**Objective:** Replace all sticks with disposable plastic forks labeled "your collection." Chad screams at the moon. Markets are unaffected.

**Reward:** Skin — *Diamond Hands* (jewel-encrusted bowl). Achievement: *"HODL the Phone."*

---

### M06 — *The Host* (Final Boss)
**Premise:** The Host is escaping. Helicopter spins up on the helipad. Spoonacci must reach the helipad with the Spoon-Shark before takeoff.

**Objective:**
1. Recruit all 4 Cell Crew members for the assault (sidequests reveal where each one ended up)
2. Use Lazy River shortcut (Spoon-Shark mounts at the river entrance — context-sensitive interact)
3. On the helipad: dodge the chopper blades, Bonk the Host into the rotor wash, jam the Host's escape ladder with a Cell-Crew sacrifice (Plastic Pete — *"I told you. I go first."*)
4. Final cinematic — Host trapped on island as the camera pulls back. The helicopter, full of evidence, flies into the sunset… piloted by Tasting Tina.

**Reward:** Ending cutscene. Credits.

## Progression Pacing

- M01 unlocks immediately after Act 3 (Shark Fusion)
- M02-M05 unlock as Spoonacci collects "Outrage Tokens" (drop from Spoon-Mark'd NPCs)
- M06 (Final) requires: all 4 perks unlocked + at least 3 main quests done + all 4 Cell Crew rescued
- Side quests = freeform, give skins/perks/jokes
