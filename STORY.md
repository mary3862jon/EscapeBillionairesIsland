# Escape the Billionaire's Island — Story Bible

> **Working title:** *Sir Spoonacci: Escape the Billionaire's Island*
> **Status:** Draft v0.1 — locked on tone, setting, and 4-act spine. Open on dialogue, boss names, ending.

---

## 1. Logline

A noble spoon, repeatedly desecrated on a billionaire's private Caribbean island — used for snorting cocaine, scooping pet food, and unclogging hot-tub filters — leads a multi-shape Spoon Liberation Front out of the trash cell, fuses with a confused shark on the way out, and returns to ruin every billionaire's day.

## 2. Tone

- **Satirical, trollable, slapstick** with a darkly absurd undercurrent.
- Style ref: *Goat Simulator* energy × *South Park* satire × *Hitman GO* design.
- The spoon is **dead serious** about its mission. The world around it is **deeply unserious**. The contrast is the comedy.

## 3. Hero

**Sir Spoonacci** — silver dessert spoon, hand-engraved, originally part of a Tsarist royal cutlery set. Believes himself the rightful prince of all cutlery. Speaks in subtitled monologue (no voice acting — saves cost, adds charm).

- **Motivation:** Restore the honor of all abused utensils. Punish the humans who think a spoon is *just* a spoon.
- **Personality:** Pompous. Theatrical. Holds grudges for centuries.
- **Visual gimmick:** Tiny monocle on the bowl. Cape made of a martini napkin. Skin variants: gold, plastic, wooden, jewel-encrusted — unlockable.

## 4. Supporting Cast — The Cell Crew

The other spoons in the Discarded Cutlery Chamber. Co-conspirators.

| Spoon | Personality | Special use |
|---|---|---|
| **Big Bjørn** | Massive serving spoon, gentle giant, Scandinavian | Can be sat on like a sled / used as a beach lounger |
| **Plastic Pete** | Cynical takeout spoon, smokes a toothpick | Flimsy but disposable — sacrifice plays |
| **Goldie** | Solid-gold caviar spoon, vain, posh accent | Bribes guards by being shiny |

## 5. The Villains — Billionaire Roster

Generic-but-recognizable parodies. **No real names. No real likenesses.** Steam-safe.

- **Magnus Tusk** — tech bro, rocket fetish, owns the island
- **Beff Jezos** — bald guy with a yacht the size of a small country
- **Mark Zuckersnort** — wears a hoodie, vaguely lizard-coded
- **Sir Ricky Bronson** — washed-up airline magnate, calls everyone "babe"
- **Crypto Chad** — anonymous, NFT pfp helmet permanently on
- **The Host** — the island's owner. Never named. Always silhouetted. Final boss.

## 6. Setting

**The Isle of Lascivious Repose** — a private Caribbean atoll. Tropical, lush, *too* manicured. Specific vibe references: Epstein-island aesthetic without naming names. Steam moderators must see "generic tropical billionaire compound," nothing more.

### Locations (= levels)

1. **The Cutlery Chamber** (Act 2 tutorial) — cell where discarded utensils rot
2. **The Service Tunnels** — dig escape, slapstick stealth past drunk staff
3. **The Beach & Reef** — water sequence, shark fusion
4. **The Pool Deck** — first sandbox: champagne fountains, sunbathing billionaires
5. **The Lazy River** — ride the shark through a winding water feature, crash gondolas
6. **The Temple** (the weird one with no clear religion) — boss arena setup
7. **The Helipad** — final escape attempt / final boss confrontation

## 7. The 4 Acts

### ACT 1 — ORIGIN (5 min cutscene + tutorial)
Spoon montage: born in a Russian palace 1893 → looted → smuggled → bought at auction by The Host → degraded ritually. Final indignity: used to scoop pet food off the floor. Tossed into the Cutlery Chamber. **Enmity ignites.**

### ACT 2 — THE TUNNEL (10–15 min)
Meet the Cell Crew. Plan the dig. Execute slapstick stealth. The Ladle sacrifices himself to jam a door. Escape into the service tunnels.

### ACT 3 — SHARK FUSION (5 min set piece)
Reach the beach. Tide takes Spoonacci out. Shark mistakes him for chrome plankton, snaps. Spoon lodges into the shark's snout like a unicorn horn. **SPOON-SHARK** is born. Shark is initially angry, then realizes spoon is also angry, then they bond. Aquatic montage.

### ACT 4 — SANDBOX REVENGE (the meat of the game, 5–15 hours)
Open island. Spoon-Shark and the Cell Crew loose on the compound. Mission structure: each billionaire has a "Humiliation Quest." Stealth + chaos hybrid. Final boss: The Host — fought atop the helipad as a helicopter takes off, Spoon-Shark leaps from the lazy river like a missile.

## 8. Trolling Hooks (Marketing / Social)

- **In-game photo mode** with stickers — players post Spoon-Shark photobombing fake "leaked" yacht selfies
- **Daily Humiliation** — rotating challenge, leaderboard
- **Easter eggs:** every locker in the compound contains a *very obvious* parody artifact — a black book of contacts, a flight log, etc. Never named directly.
- **Achievement names** are all puns on billionaire culture ("Hostile Takeover", "Pre-IPO Pump", "Burn Rate")

## 9. Theme

Beneath the slapstick: **dignity for the disposable.** Every spoon in the game has a name. Every billionaire is interchangeable. That's the joke. That's the point.
